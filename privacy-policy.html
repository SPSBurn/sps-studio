Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SPS Studio ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use any of our mobile applications ("Apps").

This policy applies to all games and applications published by SPS Studio.

Information We Collect

Our Apps do not collect, store, or share any personal information from users. We do not require registration, login, or any personal data to use our Apps.

Game progress (levels, scores, settings) is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our Apps are designed to be safe for users of all ages, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable child safety regulations.

  • We do not collect personal information from children or any users
  • We do not require registration, login, or account creation
  • We do not use tracking or analytics that identify individual users
  • All game data is stored locally on the device only

Advertising

Our Apps may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to serve ads. For Apps marked as "directed to children":

  • Only child-safe advertisements are displayed
  • Personalized advertising is disabled
  • Behavioral tracking for ad targeting is not used

For more information about Google's advertising practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

Third-Party Services

Our Apps may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • Google Play Services — for app distribution on Android
  • Apple App Store — for app distribution on iOS

Data Storage

All game data (progress, scores, preferences) is stored locally on your device using standard device storage mechanisms. No data is transmitted to external servers owned or operated by SPS Studio.

In-App Purchases

Some of our Apps may offer in-app purchases. These transactions are processed entirely by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
